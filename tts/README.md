# Text-to-Speech (TTS)

**Purpose:** Converts LLM-generated text responses into spoken audio output.

## Components

- pyttsx3 speech engine
- Voice configuration and selection
- Speech rate and volume control
- Audio playback management

## Key Responsibilities

- Initialize TTS engine
- Convert text to natural-sounding speech
- Control speech rate, pitch, and volume
- Play audio through speakers
- Support different voice profiles

## Performance Goals

- **Target latency:** 0.5-1 second
- Natural-sounding speech output
- Configurable speech rate (default: 200 WPM)
- No noticeable lag between text and audio

## Configuration Options

- Voice selection (male/female, different accents)
- Speech rate (words per minute)
- Volume control
- Pitch adjustment
